Medical Image Classifier

A convolutional network trained on the MedNIST dataset to recognize which of six imaging modalities a scan belongs to — abdomen CT, breast MRI, chest CT, chest X-ray, hand X-ray, or head CT. The model runs entirely client-side with TensorFlow.js: nothing you upload leaves your browser.

TensorFlow / Keras CNN TensorFlow.js MedNIST 99.8% val. accuracy
UPLOAD A SCAN Loading model…
What this model does: it identifies the imaging modality of a scan (which body part / scan type it is) — it does not diagnose disease and isn't a medical device. It was trained for a portfolio project, not clinical use.
