About

Sfax, Tunisia. Available for robotics & AI engineering roles.

I'm an industrial computer engineer specializing in robotics and ROS (Robot Operating System), focused on integrating AI into physical systems that solve real industrial automation problems.

My work spans the full stack of a robotic project: modeling and controllers, sensor integration (depth cameras, LiDAR, ToF), Gazebo simulation, and the computer vision and deep learning models that let a robot recognize what it's looking at and act on it. Recent work includes a wheeled humanoid pick-and-place system and autonomous navigation stacks built on Nav2 and SLAM.

Currently completing a diploma in Industrial Computer Engineering at ENET'COM, Sfax.
