Intelligent Video Surveillance — Anomaly Detection

A spatiotemporal autoencoder — Conv3D encoders feeding a stack of ConvLSTM2D layers, mirrored back out with Conv3DTranspose — trained to reconstruct normal surveillance footage. Reconstruction error on unseen clips becomes an anomaly score, evaluated on the UCSD Anomaly Dataset (Ped1/Ped2) and CUHK Avenue.

Lowest-regularity frame Where motion energy spiked most sharply relative to the rest of the clip — the frame this proxy would flag first.
What this in-browser demo actually does: the trained spatiotemporal autoencoder above needs its full TensorFlow weights and 10-frame cubes to run, so it isn't ported to the browser. Instead this widget computes a lightweight frame-difference motion-energy proxy — grayscale pixel deltas between sampled frames — and turns it into the same kind of regularity curve the notebook produces from reconstruction error. It's a stand-in for intuition, not the evaluated model.
